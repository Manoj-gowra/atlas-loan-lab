# Agricultural Equipment Financing Policy (v4)

## 1. Eligibility

### 1.1 Minimum business credit score
The farm business must have a bureau business credit score of at least 155. Scores from 140 to 154 are borderline and must be referred for investigation.

### 1.2 Debt service coverage ratio
Because of drought-driven commodity price stress, the minimum DSCR for agricultural equipment financing is raised to 1.35, including the new payment at 7.5% APR over the requested term. This replaces the 1.20 minimum in version 3. A DSCR from 1.25 to 1.34 is borderline and must be referred for investigation.

### 1.3 Loan amount limits
Agricultural equipment loans range from $25,000 to $1,500,000.

### 1.4 Collateral coverage
The LTV may not exceed 0.80 of the equipment's appraised value. An LTV from 0.80 to 0.85 is borderline and must be referred for investigation.

### 1.5 Time in business
The farm must have operated for at least 2 years.

## 2. Jurisdiction rules

### 2.1 New York
A UCC-1 financing statement must be filed with the New York Department of State before funding.

### 2.2 Texas
Texas agricultural equipment loans follow the product rules in section 1.
