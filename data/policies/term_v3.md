# Commercial Term Loan Policy (v3)

## 1. Eligibility

### 1.1 Minimum business credit score
The business must have a bureau business credit score of at least 160 on the 0-300 scale. The score is always taken from the bureau report, never from the application. Scores from 145 to 159 are borderline and must be referred for investigation rather than declined.

### 1.2 Debt service coverage ratio
The debt service coverage ratio (DSCR) is annual EBITDA divided by total annual debt service, including the estimated payments on the new loan at the standard rate over the requested term. The minimum DSCR for a term loan is 1.25. A DSCR from 1.15 to 1.24 is borderline and must be referred for investigation, where verified financials may replace stated figures.

### 1.3 Loan amount limits
Term loans range from $50,000 to $1,000,000. Jurisdiction rules in section 2 take precedence when they are stricter.

### 1.4 Collateral coverage
Term loans are secured by a lien on business assets. The loan-to-value ratio (LTV), the loan amount divided by the appraised collateral value, may not exceed 0.80. An LTV from 0.80 to 0.85 is borderline and must be referred for investigation.

### 1.5 Time in business
The business must have operated for at least 2 years. Start-ups are referred to the small business administration loan program instead.

## 2. Jurisdiction rules

### 2.1 California
For commercial financing of $500,000 or less, the California commercial financing disclosure must be delivered before the borrower signs.

### 2.2 New York
New York term loans require a personal guarantee from every owner of 20% or more of the business.

### 2.3 Texas
Texas term loans follow the product rules in section 1.

## 3. Pricing

### 3.1 Standard rate
The standard rate is 9.5% APR. Officers may not discount the rate without approval from a senior credit officer.
