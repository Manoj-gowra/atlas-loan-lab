# Commercial Real Estate Lending Policy (v3)

## 1. Eligibility

### 1.1 Minimum business credit score
The borrower must have a bureau business credit score of at least 165. This replaces the 160 minimum in version 2. Scores from 150 to 164 are borderline and must be referred for investigation.

### 1.2 Debt service coverage ratio
The minimum DSCR for commercial real estate is 1.25, including the new payment at 7.0% APR over the requested term. This replaces the 1.20 minimum in version 2. A DSCR from 1.15 to 1.24 is borderline and must be referred for investigation.

### 1.3 Loan amount limits
Commercial real estate loans range from $250,000 to $5,000,000.

### 1.4 Collateral coverage
The LTV may not exceed 0.75 of the appraised property value. Jurisdiction rules in section 2 take precedence when they are stricter. An LTV up to 0.05 above the limit is borderline and must be referred for investigation.

### 1.5 Time in business
The borrower must have operated for at least 2 years.

## 2. Jurisdiction rules

### 2.1 California
A Phase I environmental site assessment is required before funding.

### 2.2 New York
In New York the maximum LTV for commercial real estate is 0.70.

### 2.3 Texas
Texas commercial real estate loans follow the product rules in section 1.
