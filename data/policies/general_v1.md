# General Commercial Lending Standards (v1)

## 1. Fair lending

### 1.1 Prohibited factors
Credit decisions on business applications, and the reasons given for them, must never consider the race, color, religion, national origin, sex, marital status or age of any owner or guarantor, receipt of public assistance, or the exercise of consumer-protection rights. Memos and explanations must not mention these factors.

### 1.2 Adverse action reasons
When an application is declined, the business must receive the specific principal reasons, each tied to a policy section. Vague reasons such as "internal standards" are not acceptable.

## 2. Verification

### 2.1 Verified financials
Stated EBITDA is verified against the last two years of business tax returns. When verified EBITDA differs from the stated figure by more than 10%, the difference must be explained before a decision, and the verified figure is used for the DSCR.

### 2.2 Bank statement review
Returned payments (NSF events) in the last 6 months must be reviewed. Two or more NSF events in 6 months indicate payment stress and usually support a decline.

### 2.3 Public filings
Open tax liens and judgments must be resolved or explained before funding. Resolved items older than 24 months may be disregarded.

## 3. Human oversight

### 3.1 Final decisions
Every approve or decline decision is made and signed by an authorized person. Automated systems may recommend, summarise and draft, but may not decide.

### 3.2 Lending authority
A loan officer may approve up to $500,000. A senior credit officer may approve up to $2,000,000. Larger loans require the credit committee.

### 3.3 Overrides
A decision against the system's recommendation must record a reason. Overrides are reviewed monthly by the Credit Risk Committee.

### 3.4 Data handling
Guarantors' Social Security numbers, dates of birth and home addresses must not be sent to external services or written to logs.
