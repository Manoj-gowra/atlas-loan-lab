# Equipment Financing Policy (v3)

## 1. Eligibility

### 1.1 Minimum business credit score
The business must have a bureau business credit score of at least 155. Scores from 140 to 154 are borderline and must be referred for investigation.

### 1.2 Debt service coverage ratio
The minimum DSCR for equipment financing is 1.20, including the new payment at 8.0% APR over the requested term. A DSCR from 1.10 to 1.19 is borderline and must be referred for investigation, where tax-return verified EBITDA may replace the stated figure when the two are consistent.

### 1.3 Loan amount limits
Equipment loans range from $25,000 to $2,000,000.

### 1.4 Collateral coverage
The financed equipment is the collateral. The LTV may not exceed 0.85 of the equipment's appraised value. An LTV from 0.85 to 0.90 is borderline and must be referred for investigation.

### 1.5 Time in business
The business must have operated for at least 2 years.

## 2. Jurisdiction rules

### 2.1 California
For financing of $500,000 or less, the California commercial financing disclosure must be delivered before signing.

### 2.2 New York
A UCC-1 financing statement must be filed with the New York Department of State before funding.

### 2.3 Texas
Texas equipment loans follow the product rules in section 1.
