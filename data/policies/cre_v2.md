# Commercial Real Estate Lending Policy (v2, SUPERSEDED)

## 1. Eligibility

### 1.1 Minimum business credit score
The borrower must have a bureau business credit score of at least 160.

### 1.2 Debt service coverage ratio
The minimum DSCR for commercial real estate is 1.20, including the new payment at 7.0% APR over the requested term.

### 1.3 Loan amount limits
Commercial real estate loans range from $250,000 to $4,000,000.

### 1.4 Collateral coverage
The LTV may not exceed 0.80 of the appraised property value.

### 1.5 Time in business
The borrower must have operated for at least 2 years.

## 2. Jurisdiction rules

### 2.2 New York
In New York the maximum LTV for commercial real estate is 0.75.
