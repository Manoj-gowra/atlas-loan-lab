# ADR-NNN: <the decision in a few words>

Status: proposed | accepted | superseded by ADR-NNN
Date: YYYY-MM-DD · Deciders: <names and roles>

## Context
What forces this decision: the requirement or constraint, the numbers, what we observed.

## Decision
What we decided, in one or two sentences.

## Why
The reasoning a technical reader needs: mechanism, evidence (eval results, traces), constraints.

## What it gives up
The honest cost: what gets worse, what we accept, why that is acceptable. An undisclosed cost that
surfaces later is a communication failure, not a design defect.

## Alternatives considered
Each rejected option and the reason it lost.

## For executives
Two or three sentences in consequences (cost, risk, timeline vs the business outcome), no jargon.

## Revisit when
The signal that should reopen this decision.
